I'm thinking of prototyping a game similar to balatro but you are a chef prepping hotpot.

you pick different ingredients, dependin on their synergy, quality, freshness etc and the soup base and preference of customers, you get different points.

you want to re-use the soup base as much as possible whenever u add ingredient, it changes characteristic of the soup like spicier, sweeter, creamier etc. If player changes soup, they suffer drawbacks liek losing all modifiers and get points deduction

The game will eventually be made in godot but to test out the mechanics and balancing of the cards/ingredients I just want to write it in html, js and css (simple stlying) with just buttons to manually do actions.
